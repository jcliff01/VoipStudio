In this zip:
- VoipStudio.exe            the program
- vsx.exe                   the same analysis from a command line (vsx --help)
- VoipStudio-Manual.html    the manual: the program's Help pages as one page, for a browser

To use it on another PC:
- It needs the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64).
- Unzip into a folder of its own and run VoipStudio.exe. To update, close VoipStudio and unzip
  over the old folder. Settings and logs are kept elsewhere (%APPDATA%\VoipStudio) and stay.
- Playing iLBC, G.723.1 and AMR audio needs ffmpeg: on the PATH, or in an ffmpeg folder beside
  VoipStudio.exe. Everything else works without it.
